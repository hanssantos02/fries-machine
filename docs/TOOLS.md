## Gathering the strands of one 20AWG lead

For the current screw-terminal lead, the goal is simply to gather the fine copper strands from **one wire**, not to twist two separate power wires into an unsupported joint.

Keep USB and adapter disconnected and fuse removed. Strip only the terminal-fit length, gently gather strands with fingertips and follow their existing lay until no strands stick out. Stop once the bundle is neat; do not keep tightening it into a hard knot. If needed, use only a light grip of the serrated tip jaws and a small twist while supporting the insulated wire. Avoid cutting edges/notches and stop for damaged/broken strands. Support the wire rather than pulling on the assembled driver/connector.

Use the finished conductor in its intended secure screw clamp; do not solder-tin it as a substitute for a suitable termination. Do not squeeze unknown crimping slots onto the bare lead. Excessive twisting does not improve the electrical connection. No new purchase is needed merely to gather a short stranded-wire end.

The grey handles are not evidence of certified live-work insulation. Use this tool only on disconnected project wiring; no mains cutting/stripping step is issued.
