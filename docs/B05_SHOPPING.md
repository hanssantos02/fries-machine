B05 covers identification labels and hardware holding the external position switches firmly in their intended locations. It does not cover food-contact basket brackets, motor mounts or the machine frame; those belong to the mechanical team.

## Wait for physical fit

- Rigid small sensor brackets/fixtures and mounting screws/nuts/washers for the seven provisional A07 switches (four basket endpoints and three coil indexes).
- Do not assume seven new angle brackets are required: suitable existing mounting surfaces or mechanical-team fixtures may eliminate some or all separate brackets.
- Screw diameter, length, bracket size and actual quantity depend on delivered switch holes, the cam/lever alignment and the machine mounting surfaces. No guessed M2/M3/M4 purchase or large mixed screw kit yet.
- Ask the mechanical team whether it already provides these fixtures and fasteners before buying. Stable mounting is needed so switch positions do not drift; cable ties/hot glue alone are not a specified permanent alignment fixture.
